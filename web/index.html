How it works

  1. Word by word. Two small language models read your text and record how predictable every word was.
  2. Sentence by sentence. Each sentence is scored with its neighbours, like Turnitin's report.
  3. Patterns. Words, phrases and sentence shapes that AI models overuse are counted.
  4. Humanize. Flagged passages are rewritten, checked to keep their meaning, and re-scored.
